# 90-Second Procurement Walkthrough

## 0:00-0:10
Most AI demos stop after writing an answer. This agent completes a business workflow, while keeping a person accountable for the decision.

## 0:10-0:23
Here is one synthetic purchase request, three supplier quotations, and the company’s award policy. The agent receives the same messy inputs a procurement team normally opens one by one.

## 0:23-0:45
It extracts price, delivery, payment, validity, warranty, and compliance. Then it applies mandatory gates and scores eligible suppliers. The cheapest offer fails warranty and delivery requirements, so price alone cannot win.

## 0:45-1:02
The agent recommends Cedar Industrial. It shows why, cites the exact records, exposes every policy check, and labels its confidence. Nothing is hidden behind a vague AI answer.

## 1:02-1:16
The workflow stops here. A procurement manager can approve, return it for clarification, or escalate the exception. The agent does not make the consequential action on its own.

## 1:16-1:25
After approval, it prepares the award record and logs the full path from intake to human decision. No external system is changed in this public proof.
